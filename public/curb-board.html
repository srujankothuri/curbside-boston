How this board works
  • Items are newest first. "Fresh" means posted in the last 24 hours. After 3 days, an item is marked "May be gone."
  • Picked something up, or found it gone? Mark it as taken so nobody else makes the trip.
  • Your own posts show Edit and Delete. They're tied to this browser instead of an account, so clearing your browser data means you can't edit them anymore.
  • Filters apply right away and stay in the page address, so you can share a filtered link.
