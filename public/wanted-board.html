How this board works
  • Requests are newest first. Each one lists the neighborhoods the person can pick up from, and how to reach them.
  • Have something a person needs? Contact them directly using the details on their request.
  • Your own requests show Mark as found, Edit, and Delete. They're tied to this browser instead of an account.
  • Filters apply right away and stay in the page address, so you can share a filtered link.
