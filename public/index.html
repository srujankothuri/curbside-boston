How it works

  1. Spot it or leave it

    Moving out, or walking past a good chair on the sidewalk? Either works.

  2. Post it in seconds

    Add what it is, its condition, and the street. No account needed.

  3. Mark it taken when it's gone

    Picked it up or found it gone? One tap saves the next person a trip.

Looking for something specific?

Post a request on the Wanted Board with what you need and where you can pick it up. People clearing out their place can find you.

Good to know

  • No account needed. You can edit or delete your posts from the same browser you posted them from.
  • Never include house numbers. A street or cross street is enough.
  • Covers Boston's neighborhoods, plus Cambridge and Brookline.
